## Why not a permissive licence?

The business model is deliberately undecided. Loosening a licence later is
trivial; tightening one after outside contributions have landed requires
tracking down and getting agreement from every contributor. This preserves the
option at close to zero cost to legitimate users.

## Known cost

pub.dev's scoring penalises non-OSI licences and will report this package's
licence as unrecognised. This is a real and accepted adoption cost.
