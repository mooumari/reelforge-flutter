## 0.1.0

First release.

Ready-made scenes, charts and motion primitives for FlutterMotion
compositions. Every component was extracted from the example reel rather than
designed in advance.

- `Storyboard` and `Scene` — declare lengths in seconds, derive start frames.
- `SceneTransition` — fade, slide, scale, or cut.
- `Enter` and `Stagger` — four ways for something to arrive, and a way to make
  a list arrive one item at a time.
- `Counter` — a number that counts, as a pure function of the frame.
- `BarChart`, `LineChart`, `StatCard`, `CardGrid`.
- `TitleCard`, `SceneLabel`, `LabelledScene`, `BigStat`, `BigStatList`.
- `FootageOverlay` and `SplitScreen`.
- `MotionTheme`, `MotionPalette`, `MotionTypography`, `MotionSurface`.
